Theoretical probability

The expected chance under the model. A fair coin has a 50% chance of heads on each flip.

Observed frequency

The number of successes divided by the number of trials actually simulated. It can differ from the theoretical value.

Independent trials

Each simulated result is generated independently. Previous results do not change the next trial's probability.
